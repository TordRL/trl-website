01 — About

A short introduction

IT consultant with hands-on experience across the Microsoft ecosystem — Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune, Dynamics 365 and Power Platform. Bachelor's in IT and information systems from UiA (2017). Most of my career has been at Norsk Psykologforening, where I've driven several modernisation projects independently from idea to production.

I've helped systematise workflows in an organisation that had outgrown Excel and personal inboxes — a modular ticket system on SharePoint and Power Platform, an endpoint migration from GPO to Intune, automated case-handling processes, and the internal training around Microsoft's move to real-time marketing in Dynamics 365. I reach for the simplest thing that actually solves the problem.
