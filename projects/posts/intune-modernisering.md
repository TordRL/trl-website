In the winter of 2025 I picked up a project that had been frozen for some time and got it moving again. The goal was to migrate around 80 machines from traditional Group Policy (GPO) to Microsoft Intune — modernising endpoint management and lining up with the broader cloud migration the organisation was already in the middle of.

I drove the work alone from the organisation's side, in collaboration with our managed service provider, and completed the move over roughly two months.

**Approach:**

- Reviewed the existing GPO setup and decided which policies to migrate, replace, or retire
- Configured the Intune environment and built out device-management policies
- Coordinated the rollout in phases so it didn't disrupt day-to-day operations
- Documented the new enrollment process so onboarding a device is now a routine task

**Results:**

- ~80 machines migrated from GPO to Intune over two months
- Centralised endpoint management with a single pane of glass
- New machine enrolment now costs roughly NOK 3,000 less per device, against about 30 minutes of internal time
- Closed out a project that had been stalled for an extended period
